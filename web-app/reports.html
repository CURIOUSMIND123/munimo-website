Work-in-Progress Statement
Percentage-of-completion (cost-to-cost) method per AS-7: % complete = cost incurred to date ÷ the site's budgeted estimated cost. Recognized revenue is the contract value scaled by that %. Comparing recognized revenue to what's actually been billed shows under-billing (work done ahead of invoicing — an asset) or over-billing (invoiced ahead of work done — a liability). Set a site's Estimated Cost on its edit screen to enable this for that site.
