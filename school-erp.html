Questions

School ERP, answered.

Is the demo the actual school software?
It is a working school system with sample data — collect a fee and the dues drop and a receipt prints, mark a class and the attendance percentage moves. Your build starts from this and then changes, because fee structures, report card formats and academic calendars are never the same at two institutions.
Can parents pay fees online?
Yes, through a payment gateway connected during the build. Receipts are generated automatically and the dues list updates itself.
We are a coaching institute, not a school. Does that work?
Yes. Batches instead of classes, flexible fee cycles and shorter courses are all normal configurations rather than special cases.
What about our existing student data?
It comes across. Spreadsheets, an old system, or a mix of both — we map and import it, then run a checking period before you rely on it.

Tell us how your institution runs.

Send us your fee structure and a sample report card. That is usually enough for us to tell you what a build would involve.
