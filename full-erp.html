Questions

Full business ERP, answered.

Is this only for construction?
No. The engines underneath — ledger, tax, payroll, stock, approvals — are the same for any business. The demo happens to be seeded with a construction company because that is where we started, but we run it for trading, services, manufacturing and institutions. The fields and workflows get changed to match your trade.
Can it replace what we use now?
Usually yes. We import your masters and opening balances, run the old and the new side by side for a period, and only switch when the numbers match and your team is comfortable. Nobody is asked to switch on a promise.
How many people can use it?
As many as you need, each with their own login and their own permissions. What a person can see and do is set by role, and every entry records who made it.
Does it work outside India?
Yes. Multi-currency, multi-tax and multi-language are built in, and the statutory formats are configured per country during the build.
