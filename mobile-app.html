Questions

Mobile field app, answered.

Does it need an app store download?
No. It installs to the home screen straight from the browser and behaves like a normal app after that. Updates arrive on their own, so nobody is stuck on an old version.
What if a phone is lost or a person leaves?
Access is a login you control, not something tied to the device. Revoke it and the data stays where it belongs.
How long can it stay offline?
Indefinitely for practical purposes. It holds what it captured and syncs when it can.
Can field staff see the company accounts?
Only what you allow. By default they see their own site and their own submissions, never the full books.
