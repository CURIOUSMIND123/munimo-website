Questions

Cash Book, answered.

Is this an accounting package or just a register?
It is a real double-entry ledger underneath, presented as a simple book. You get the simplicity of a register and books that an accountant can actually work from — including a trial balance that balances.
Can my site staff use it without training?
That is the point of the phone version. Two large buttons for money in and money out, and plain-language entry for anyone who will not fill in a form. The office keeps control through approvals.
What happens with no internet?
The phone app keeps recording and syncs when signal returns. Nothing is lost and nothing is entered twice.
Can it grow into the full ERP later?
Yes, and your data comes with you. The cash book is the same ledger the full ERP uses, so moving up is a change of screens, not a re-entry of history.
